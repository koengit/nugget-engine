Compute $1$, $1 + 3$, $1 + 3 + 5$, $1 + 3 + 5 + 7$. What do you notice? Can you explain it?

## Hint
Draw the numbers as L-shaped layers of dots.

## Answer
You get the [[squares]] $1, 4, 9, 16$: $1 + 3 + \dots + (2n-1) = n^2$. Each odd number $2k-1$ is an L-shaped layer that grows a $(k-1) \times (k-1)$ square of dots into a $k \times k$ square.
